How do I get books in?

Drag EPUBs or PDFs into the library (Mac), use the Import button or the Files app (iPhone/iPad), or send a file to Marginalia from any app's share sheet. Paper books can be shelved too — add one and capture quotes with the camera as you read. On iOS you can also browse free public-domain catalogs from inside the app.

How do I save web articles?

Paste a link anywhere in the library and Marginalia fetches a clean, readable copy. You can also clip from the built-in browser, or send a page via the share sheet.

An article from a subscription site only captured a teaser

Paywalled sites need you signed in inside Marginalia once: open the built-in browser, log in to the site, then press Clip. Marginalia remembers the session for future captures from that site.

How does sync work?

Through your own iCloud — no Marginalia account exists. Sign both devices into the same Apple ID with iCloud enabled for Marginalia, and your library, highlights, and notebooks follow you. Give a fresh install a few minutes to pull a large library down.

Setting up AI features (bring your own key)

Marginalia's AI is strictly optional and uses your API key. Create a key with Anthropic or OpenAI, paste it in Settings → AI, and it's stored in your device's Keychain. Your text goes directly from your device to the provider — never through us. Everything else in the app works without any key.

Read-aloud voices

The system voice and the neural Kokoro voice run entirely on your device. If you've added an OpenAI or ElevenLabs key, you can pick their voices too. Word-by-word highlighting follows along as it reads.

Getting my notes out

Export annotations as formatted text from any book, or (on iOS) send them to your own Notion workspace. Your data is never locked in.

The free trial and unlocking

Marginalia is free to try for 5 days with everything included. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks it for good; there is no subscription. The iPhone and iPad app and the Mac app are separate purchases. Already bought it on a new device? Open the unlock screen or Settings and tap Restore Purchases. If the trial ends before you decide, you can still export everything you made.
